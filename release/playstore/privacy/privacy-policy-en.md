# AZIZ Privacy Policy

## Summary

AZIZ is a party game you play with friends on one phone. It has no accounts, no
developer-operated servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The app keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you left it (the language
you picked, whether vibration is on, the timer you chose, and the player names you typed
in). That information stays on your phone. **AZIZ does not collect or receive any data
from your device**, and the developer has no way to see it.

## Information stored locally on your device

AZIZ stores the following in its private app storage on your phone:

- **Player names** — the names you type in before a game so you do not have to enter them
  again next time. You can remove any name from the players list inside the app at any time.
- **Settings** — your language (Armenian or English), whether vibration feedback is on, and
  the timer length you picked for Wrong Answer Only.

That is all. This information is used only to run the game on your phone. It is not sent
anywhere by AZIZ, it is not linked to any account (there are none), and the developer does
not receive it.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's
settings or by uninstalling the app.

## What AZIZ does not do

- **No account or sign-in.** There is nothing to register for.
- **No developer server or backend.** AZIZ does not talk to any service run by the developer.
- **No analytics or crash-reporting.** No usage statistics or diagnostic reports are collected.
- **No advertising.** The app contains no ads and no advertising SDK, and it does not use
  the Android advertising ID.
- **No tracking.** Nothing identifies you or follows you across apps or websites.
- **No sale or sharing of data.** There is no data to sell or share.
- **No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, phone or SMS.**
  The app does not request these permissions.

## Network behaviour and permissions

AZIZ works fully offline. Every game, every question and every card is included in the app
itself; nothing is downloaded during play and no gameplay data is uploaded.

The app declares two Android permissions:

- **INTERNET** — a standard permission included by the framework AZIZ is built on. The
  version 1.0.0 app does not use it to send or receive your data; there is no server for it
  to contact.
- **VIBRATE** — used for short vibration feedback during countdowns and button presses.
  You can turn this off in Settings.

(Android also generates an app-specific "signature" permission for every installed app;
this is a system mechanism and does not give AZIZ access to any of your data.)

## Android backups

Like most Android apps, AZIZ's local preferences may be included in the device backups that
Android performs through **your own Google account** (Android Auto Backup), if you have
backups enabled on your phone. This is an operating-system feature under your control and
governed by Google's terms and privacy policy. The developer of AZIZ does not receive, see
or have access to those backups. You can manage or disable app backups in your phone's
Google / backup settings.

## Children

AZIZ is a general-audience social game intended for teenagers and adults playing together;
it is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect any personal
information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children
either. If you believe a child has entered information into the app, you can delete it by
removing the name in the players list or by clearing the app's storage.

## Security

Because AZIZ never transmits your information, there is nothing in transit to protect. The
data described above lives in the app's private storage on your phone and is protected by
your device's own security (screen lock, encryption and app sandboxing provided by
Android). Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see the saved player names,
so treat the device as you would with any other app.
