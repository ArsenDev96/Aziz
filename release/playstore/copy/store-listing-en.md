## Full description

```
AZIZ is a collection of party games for friends, played together on one phone. Pass it around the table, pick a game and start — no accounts, no internet needed, nothing to set up.

Three games are included.

WRONG ANSWER ONLY
Simple questions, one rule: never say the right answer. You have a few seconds, and the group decides whether you made it. Harder than it sounds.

SAME ANSWER
One prompt for the whole team. Everyone silently picks an answer, then says it out loud at the same time. The more of you who match, the more points you score.

ACT IT!
Teams take turns. One teammate guesses while the others act out the word — no talking. Guess as many as you can before the timer runs out.

Good to know
• Fully offline — nothing in the game needs an internet connection
• No account or sign-up
• Armenian and English, switchable at any time
• Player names are typed in once and kept on your phone
• 2–8 players (the team games need at least 4), quick rounds, rules explained in the app

Made for game nights, birthdays, trips and long evenings with friends.
```
