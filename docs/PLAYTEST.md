# Phase 5 — first real playtest

Do not fix anything until you have watched one full session. Your job in the room is to
**watch**, not to explain.

## Setup

- 3–6 people, sitting together, one phone.
- Hand the phone over with no explanation beyond "it's a game, press play".
- Armenian is the default language. Switch to English from Settings if the group prefers it.

## What to write down

| Question | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Do people laugh? | In the 3 seconds, or after the verdict? |
| Is 3 seconds right? | Count how often the timer runs out with nothing said. |
| Are the questions too easy? | Does anyone actually blurt the real answer? |
| Which questions land? | Note ids from the top of the screen — the boring ones get cut. |
| Is passing the phone annoying? | Watch the gap between turns. |
| Do they argue about PASS/FAIL? | If yes, the judging rule needs work. |
| Does the score matter? | Does anyone look at the results screen? |
| How long is one session? | Start to winner, in minutes. |
| Do they want another round? | An unprompted "again!" is the signal we are looking for. |

## After the session

Send me: the timer verdict, the questions to cut, the session length, and whether anyone asked
for another round. That decides Phase 6.
